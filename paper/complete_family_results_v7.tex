\subsection{What complete family optimization adds}
\textbf{Broader policy search changes the fixed-view answer.}
The 107 previously unresolved primary finite Fusion cases split into 86
above-threshold and 21 at-most-threshold family answers. Exact reoptimization
of the same three policies recovers none: all 86 gains require different
policies, rather than tighter transferred certificates.
The 21 exclusions have $0<\mathcal R\le\delta$, so positivity alone would
not justify continuing search. Together with the earlier 12 above-threshold
and 121 zero-capacity cases, the totals are 98 and 142 out of 240.
Across all 86 recovered cases, the attainable gain has median $0.991$\,pp,
interquartile range $[0.583,1.549]$\,pp and range $[0.105,3.632]$\,pp.
For every one, the three-policy optimum after including the reference is zero.
These are robust guarantees under the same view; the changed input is the policy set, not the information.

Both methods agree on all 2,160 unique synthetic requests (5,760 memberships).
Their 4,320 responses, 7,762 fixed-policy optima, 220 Coat calls and 468 timing
calls pass independent verification: 12,770/12,770, without timeout or
incomplete call (Fig.~\ref{fig:complete-family}).

\textbf{Exact answers with dimension-dependent cost.}
All 234 timing pairs succeed. Across three rounds, the construction/SoPlex
geometric-mean service ratios are $0.808$--$0.835$ at $d=8$,
$1.066$--$1.129$ at $d=64$, and $1.170$--$1.196$ at $d=256$.
The construction is slower in 5/78, 55/78 and 54/78 pairs, respectively.
These compare checked service for a Python rational construction and compiled rational LP. Pair costs, certificate sizes, memory observations and failure denominators are archived.
Table~\ref{tab:family-absolute} reports absolute costs. At $d=256$, verification
alone takes median 144.3/144.6\,ms (Part/LP), with about 149.5\,kB per certificate.
These pooled medians describe evidence cost, not matched-call ratios.

\textbf{Separating policy choice from source information.}
At the larger conservative budget, projected R passes 223/240 thresholds
versus Fusion's 218/240; R has greater capacity in 128 pairs, Fusion in 100,
with 12 ties. At the smaller oracle-boundary budget, OBS and Fusion both pass
239 thresholds, yet OBS has greater capacity in 219 pairs and Fusion in 21.
Their full- and split-budget views are not nested. All 24 cells and exact
decompositions remain available. In the partition study's 2,880 finite views,
the generating means satisfy the view and every returned-policy assertion is
correct against the stored truth. These retrospective checks do not establish
uniform coverage or validate the source assumptions.
