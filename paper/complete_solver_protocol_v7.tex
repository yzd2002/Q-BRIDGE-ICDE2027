\subsection{A complete exact solver and the source of recovery}
SoPlex 8.1.0 in rational mode~\cite{SoPlex2026,Gleixner2016Refinement,Gleixner2020ExactLP} solves the epigraph LP on identical $(\C,w,\pi_0)$:
\begin{equation}
 \min_{m\in\C,t}\ \sum_xw_x\bigl(t_x-\pi_{0,x}^{\top}m_x\bigr),
 \qquad m_{xa}\le t_x,\quad t_x\text{ free}.
 \label{eq:generic-family-lp}
\end{equation}
Stationarity of free $t_x$ gives $\sum_a\mu_{xa}=w_x$ for its nonnegative epigraph multipliers. Thus $\pi_{xa}=\mu_{xa}/w_x$ is a policy when $w_x>0$; use the reference row when $w_x=0$. Original-row duals and a feasible point complete the partition method's certificate contract. Import/export and checking are exact; neither the construction's answer nor a saved point is supplied.

A value-independent hash selects 78 requests for three paired rounds, alternating method order with fresh models and workers. Service time covers transmission after worker initialization through certificate checking, including serialization; startup/cleanup are reported separately. Limits are one CPU thread, ten seconds per service and 8\,GiB address space. Failures retain their costs and denominators. Repeated rounds measure machine variation, not new datasets.

To determine whether recovery comes from better bounds or a different policy, we solve the original three policies exactly, obtaining optima $L_i^*$. Set $L_3^+=\max(0,L_1^*,L_2^*,L_3^*)$, with zero from the reference. Here $L_{\rm saved}$ is the maximum of zero and saved lower bounds for these same three policies. Then
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal R-L_{\rm saved}
 =\underbrace{L_3^+-L_{\rm saved}}_{\text{certificate-transfer gap}}
 +\underbrace{\mathcal R-L_3^+}_{\text{three-policy restriction}}.
 \label{eq:recovery-decomposition}
\end{equation}
Both terms are nonnegative. Exact policy normalization can loosen saved bounds; missing optima leave the decomposition unknown. The recovery set and threshold stay fixed.
