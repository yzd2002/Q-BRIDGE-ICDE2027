\paragraph{Catalogue queries with ordinary caching}
At primary $(\Lambda,\rho)=(2,0)$, each of five source views receives an extended
catalogue combining two indicators:
$\pi_i\propto t_1^{h_{1i}}t_2^{h_{2i}}$,
$(t_1,t_2)\in\{.5,1,2\}^2\setminus\{(1,1)\}$.
Ten pairs give 80 policy positions at margins $0,.001,.01$ (0, 0.1, 1\,pp):
240 requests per view, 50 objectives and 150 objective--margin pairs.
Prepared execution retains exact dual terms; optimized-exact adds sparse
arithmetic and cached number conversions. Both fully cache objectives and retain a persistent receiver.
Native hot-start retains a solver basis without cross-objective certificate candidates.
Quality checks precede three timing rounds.

Prepared and optimized-exact agree on all 1,200 requests.
Optimized/prepared cost ratios $0.960$, $0.941$, $0.953$ miss the ten-percent
target; optimized-exact is slower in 3/15 view-rounds and retains more
estimated exact-state payload in every view (not isolated RSS).
Native hot-start changes 98 false answers to unknown and four unknown answers
to false, never reversing a Boolean answer. Overall service differs;
on R projected, decisions agree and native is faster in all rounds.
None certifies improvement; neither fusion-only information target is supported.
All five primary views have zero capacity, already checkable by a
constant-action witness.
