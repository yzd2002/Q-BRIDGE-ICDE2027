\textbf{Stopping under the primary declaration.}
All five primary $\Lambda=2,\rho=0$ views have zero capacity.
Group Fusion permits all item means to equal $0.2186$ (rounded), tying every
policy with the reference. This checked constant point justifies stopping
without full optimization. The stronger full-cell control also has zero capacity.

\textbf{Searching beyond candidates under other declarations.}
With ideal uniform reference $1/300$, exact family optimization resolves
60 grid positions: 49 have $\mathcal R=0$, four $0<\mathcal R\le.001$,
and seven $\mathcal R>.001$.
The seven positive positions are five unique requests: OBS at $\Lambda=1$
repeats across three tolerances; group Fusion passes at $\Lambda=1$ for all
three tolerances and at $\Lambda=1.5,\rho=0$.
The original fifteen candidates certified none. Guarantees remain conditional
on the stated assumptions, without joint coverage across declarations.

\textbf{A redesign under stronger assumptions ($\Lambda=1.5,\rho=0$).}
The jacket-type certificate moves all 28 vests' uniform mass to item 0,
a men's bomber: its probability rises from $1/300$ to $29/300$;
vests receive zero and 271 other items retain $1/300$.
All 15 original and 50 extended catalogue policies retain full support;
none makes this choice. The contrast is
$(28/300)(m_0-\overline m_{\rm vest})$, where $\overline m_{\rm vest}$ is
the 28 vests' uniform mean. Its checked lower bound is
$(28/300)(\ell_0-b_{\rm vest}/\kappa_{\rm vest})$:
row mass $\kappa$ converts the group constraint to its uniform mean.
The only nonzero terms, $2.472638$\,pp and $-2.226283$\,pp, sum to
$0.246355$\,pp in expected high-rating probability over uniform.
This exceeds $0.1$\,pp and matches the full overlapping view's capacity;
matched full-budget OBS and both R views have zero capacity.
This retrospective guarantee assumes confounding no greater than $\Lambda=1.5$
and no group-mean drift. It neither selects assumptions from data nor measures
purchase uplift, and does not imply every vest is worse.

Subview capacity cannot exceed its parent's, as relaxation requires.
These are dependent views of one dataset, not 144 independent experiments
