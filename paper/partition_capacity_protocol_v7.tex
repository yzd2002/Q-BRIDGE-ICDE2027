\subsection{Whole-family queries on fixed views}
We reuse the 48 main worlds with five samples each and target $\delta=.001$. Three source constructions (OBS, projected R, projected Fusion), two R sample sizes, two radii and finite/population information give 24 comparison cells. Population views remove sampling noise using the known simulator statistics, while retaining uncertainty allowed by the source assumptions. The $48\times5=240$ sampled datasets yield 5,760 comparison positions. Both methods solve the 2,160 distinct exact $(\C,w,\pi_0)$ problems; all positions remain in comparisons.

The primary recovery study uses projected Fusion, $100d$ randomized events and $\Lambda=2$. It searches the 107 of 240 finite views unresolved by saved evidence, keeping data and thresholds fixed. We first normalize and recheck the three saved policies exactly. This retrospective search uses Theorem~\ref{thm:simultaneous}'s coverage for adaptive selection. We also read the same exact capacities at larger thresholds, without refitting or changing the 107-view subset.
