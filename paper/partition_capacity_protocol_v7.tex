\subsection{Whole-family queries on fixed views}
We reuse the 48 main worlds with five samples each and target $\delta=.001$. Three source constructions (OBS, projected R, projected Fusion), two R sample sizes, two radii and finite/population information give 24 comparison cells. Population views remove sampling noise using the known simulator statistics, while retaining uncertainty allowed by the source assumptions. The $48\times5=240$ sampled datasets yield 5,760 comparison positions. These reduce to 2,160 distinct exact $(\C,w,\pi_0)$ problems, each solved by both methods. Deduplication changes computation, not the compared positions.

The primary recovery study uses projected Fusion, $100d$ randomized events and $\Lambda=2$. It searches the 107 of 240 finite views unresolved by saved evidence, keeping data and thresholds fixed. We first normalize and recheck the three saved policies exactly. This retrospective search uses Theorem~\ref{thm:simultaneous}'s coverage for adaptive policy selection, rather than a new out-of-sample test.
