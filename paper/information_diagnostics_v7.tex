\textbf{Sampling uncertainty versus ambiguity.}
Finite views include sampling intervals; population views use exact generating statistics, retaining ambiguity. For the metadata policy, all 240 true gains exceed $\delta$: 12 queries are certified, 195 are limited by ambiguity and 33 by sampling uncertainty (Table~\ref{tab:fresh}). Exact optima exclude optimization failure. Six additional finite certifications comprise four with sufficient standalone population information and two requiring fusion. Robust design retains the reference in 238/240 lower-budget cases: true effect zero.

For the metadata policy, Fusion adds/loses 6/0, 9/0, 0/2 and 3/1 for $(\Lambda=2,100d)$, $(2,1000d)$, $(\Lambda^*,100d)$ and $(\Lambda^*,1000d)$, respectively; oracle $\Lambda^*$ comes from simulator truth. In the low-budget oracle case, constraint gain averages 0.0114\,pp versus 0.0913\,pp budget cost. The zero-gain test holds for 193/240 queries, including both losses; the larger-budget loss has positive gain below cost. All three losses persist at exact optima. Different samples and design policies prevent a causal sample-size interpretation; full diagnostics are archived.
