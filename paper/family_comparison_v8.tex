\par\textbf{Classical allocation.} Continuous maximin sharing~\cite[Sec.~2.2]{Yamada1998Sharing}
solves $\max_{0\le z\le1}\min_g\sum_{i\in G_g}v_i z_i$ under one resource
budget, recovering capacities and allocations. Its groups partition profits;
ours impose disjoint two-sided moments on means. We minimize
$\max_i m_i-p^\top m$ per context and recover original-constraint certificates.
The general exact LP supplies the same family answer and certificate contract.
