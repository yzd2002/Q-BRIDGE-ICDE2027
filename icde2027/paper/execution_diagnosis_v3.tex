\textbf{Which reusable evidence helps?} All 41 earlier-history hits use primal points; none uses an earlier dual. Of these, 38 occur in the 64-cell disjoint, unrelated-query region, where median joint ratios are 0.909 against Threshold Hot and 0.907 against Auto. The other three hits occur in two regions without consistent improvement against both controls. Across the nine nearby-query engine regions, 2,673 answers use the last dual and 27 invoke the solver; earlier history never participates. Dual History and Threshold Hot have identical routes and verdicts on all 6,000 queries, as do Both Histories and Primal History. These are observed sequence comparisons, not a validated routing rule.

Solves alone poorly describe cost. For Both Histories over the complete mixed workload, producer verification plus the entire receiver account for 87.9\% of joint time; native solving accounts for 0.173\%. Primal preparation is nested inside verification and is not added again. Complete responses average 51,493 uncompressed bytes, including metadata. Thus additional saved solves need not offset checking and service overhead. These measurements do not isolate a causal effect of any single component or include network latency.
